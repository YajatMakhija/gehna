# Gehna
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Medusa v2 commerce backend, Next.js storefront, PostgreSQL, Redis, Cash on Delivery and Shiprocket. Medusa is explicitly requested; the storefront follows the accepted implementation plan.

## Users
Customers buying jewellery, and the single brand's owner and staff managing products, fixed prices, stock and orders.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Single brand, fixed prices. Product browsing, search, bag, email/password customer accounts, Cash on Delivery checkout and an authenticated merchant dashboard. Customers can rate and review delivered purchases, update their single review per product, and request returns from their account. Customers supply an email and mobile number; SMS verification is not required. Shiprocket provides courier booking and labels. The staff assistant creates draft listings from product photos for review before publishing, and cancels eligible unshipped COD orders after confirmation. No multivendor payouts or metal-rate pricing.

## Brand Commitments
Use the supplied Gehna logo and its black-and-gold identity.

## Evidence on Hand
User-supplied logo. No actual product catalog, prices, materials, policies or business contact details supplied. Seed catalog and generated photographs are illustrative samples, explicitly disclosed in preview mode. No fabricated reviews or certification claims.

## Open Decisions
Real catalog, final shipping rate, returns policy, tax configuration and production service credentials.
